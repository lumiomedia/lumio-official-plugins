# Changelog

## 0.1.4

- An episode or film you are still watching is no longer marked watched by the sync. The service marks a title watched at 80 % while you play it, and the sync used to bring that back as a finished viewing, so Continue watching jumped to the next episode and started it from the beginning. A viewing you finish later on another device is still imported.

## 0.1.2

- Continue watching: titles paused in other apps (the service's paused positions) now show in Lumio's Continue watching. Needs Lumio 0.1.636 or later.

## 0.1.1

- Connecting an account with a large watch history no longer freezes Lumio: the first sync now saves your history in one go instead of title by title. Most noticeable on TV boxes.

## 0.1.0

- Connect with a device code (QR on TV).
- Scrobble, two-way sync of watched titles and watchlist, SIMKL status lists and "Trending on Simkl" as home rows.
